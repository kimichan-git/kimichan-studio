Social Media Posts

Overview

Social media post designs for campaigns and ongoing brand presence. Formats adapt to platform needs while keeping a coherent visual language and readable hierarchy.

Role & deliverables

  • Post templates and campaign key visuals
  • Typography and colour systems for feed use
  • Multi-format adaptations (square, story, landscape)
  • Asset handoff for scheduling and publishing

Approach

Strong focal points, limited text, and consistent margins keep posts legible on mobile while supporting a recognisable brand presence over time.
