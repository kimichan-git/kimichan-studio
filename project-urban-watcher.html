VRInteractive

Urban Watcher

Designed as an immersive, first-person experience, this interactive artwork encourages audiences to reconsider familiar surroundings that are at risk of disappearing. The project unfolds across sites selected for their unique character, historical significance, and emotional resonance within the community. By transforming viewers into active participants, the piece emphasizes the importance of recognizing and valuing the transient aspects of our physical world.

Field Trip

The field trip will evaluate site feasibility. Locations are subject to adjustment based on our assessment, and the final schedule will supersede the initial itinerary.

Conference with advisor

Alongside the field trip, three meetings with my advisor, Adam Tam, greatly shaped this project. His insights prompted me to delve into imagery and sound research, which I mapped out in detailed notes. A key breakthrough occurred during our second session, where we tackled the technical design. Rather than leaving the audience as passive viewers looking at tablets, we brainstormed ways to build a truly immersive experience using virtual reality concepts.

Production

To execute the project, I secured key equipment through the school, including a RICOH THETA S camera, a Zoom H3-VR Audio Recorder, and a 3DVista Virtual Tour software license.

In the next phase, the images were retouched and audio noise reduction was applied. The processed media will now be imported into the software to build the virtual tour.

Outcome
