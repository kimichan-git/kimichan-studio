Urban Watcher

Overview

Urban Watcher is an immersive virtual reality experience that invites people to slow down and notice the everyday city environments they usually pass by. Through layered image and sound, the project turns ordinary streets and spaces into a reflective visual journey.

Role & deliverables

  • Concept and visual direction
  • Image and sound integration for an immersive experience
  • Exhibition and production support
  • Interactive storytelling across digital media

Approach

The project balances the energy of the city with quieter, more abstract moments. Field documentation, exhibition setup, and iterative testing shaped a final experience that feels both authentic and contemplative.
