Camping Day

A strategy board game and packaging design created during COVID-19 to bring the relaxing experience of outdoor camping into people's homes.

Graphic Design Printing Product Design

Overview

Conceived in Hong Kong during COVID-19 travel restrictions, this original board game design was created as a way to bring the experience of nature into the home. Camping serves as the central theme, delivering a relaxing, social atmosphere built around territory acquisition, skill cards, and clear win conditions.

Gameplay

Set in the suburbs, players build tents, expand territory, and collect tools. Each round, draw skill cards to construct tents. After four tents, players may draw function cards to protect their camp or demolish others. The winner is who builds the most tents — scored by points.

The map shows 32 strongholds in a bird’s-eye view. Four corners are starting points; each player picks a color (red, yellow, green, or blue). Small tents need two skill cards; big tents need three. A big tent is worth two points, a small tent one. Play runs clockwise or counterclockwise; two to four cards may be used per round.

Game assets

Full component set: 64 chess pieces (triangular tent shapes, 16 per color), 84 skill cards (camping tools for redeeming tents), and 82 function cards (attack or defend with specific use conditions). Plus a user manual for rules and setup.

Packaging design

The cover uses an iconic Hong Kong background and public estates to ground the camping theme locally. The reverse carries instructional content. A durable postal box stores the many cards and small pieces, with a sleek surface suited to the graphic system.

Production

Paper cards were printed in Mong Kok, then cropped by hand. The full set was assembled as a finished prototype over the 25-day production.
