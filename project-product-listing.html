Product Listing Graphics

Overview

A set of product listing and promotional graphics for online retail. The work emphasises hierarchy, accurate product representation, and readable value messaging at thumbnail and full sizes.

Role & deliverables

  • Main and secondary listing images
  • Promotional banners and sales graphics
  • Typography and layout for small-size clarity
  • Consistent brand treatment across assets

Approach

E-commerce graphics must work at a glance. Composition, contrast, and a clear value line help shoppers understand the offer quickly in a crowded marketplace.
