Setup Video

Overview

Setup video content that explains product installation or first-time use. Visual steps, simple captions, and a calm pace help viewers follow along without friction.

Role & deliverables

  • Step-by-step shot list and framing
  • Filming of setup sequences
  • Editing with clear chapter beats
  • Captions and instructional graphics

Approach

Clarity comes first: one action per beat, consistent camera angles, and minimal distraction so users can complete the setup with confidence.
