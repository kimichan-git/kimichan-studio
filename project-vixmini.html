VIXMINI Product Photography

Overview

Product photography and visual direction for VIXMINI, a beauty brand focused on hair tools. The work highlights product form, finish, and lifestyle context so the brand feels confident and approachable.

Role & deliverables

  • Product photography
  • Lighting and styling direction
  • Retouching and colour consistency
  • Visual assets for digital and e-commerce use

Approach

Clean, soft lighting and careful composition keep the focus on product quality while supporting a warm, confident brand tone suitable for online retail and social channels.
