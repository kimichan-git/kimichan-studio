Overview

VIXMINI is a hair care enterprise specializing in hair straighteners and curlers. Operative since 2022, it is a sub-brand of GL.iNet. Its goal is to give customers confidence and contentment through superior products. Amazon is the primary sales platform.

I was responsible for designing listing images and editing demo videos — inviting models for shoots, producing premium listing photography, and crafting video content that is visually captivating and effective in promoting the brand.

Focus

The work spans product photography, video, and Amazon-ready graphic design — turning a new hair tool into a clear, trustworthy listing experience.

01

Listing clarity

Show functionality and features at a glance — lock button, lights, floating plate — so shoppers understand the product fast.

Storyboard & shoot

Before filming, the marketing team builds a full storyboard: prop list, time estimates, visual references, and a rundown of key moments. That document is the production guide on set.

Each model is briefed on the video structure and walked through product use step by step. To stay efficient in a tight schedule, models arrive with makeup already applied.

Video editing

After capture, footage is reviewed and the strongest takes are selected for the opening video (horizontal) and demo videos (vertical). Color and exposure are balanced for a clean, consistent look.

The brand logo and background music are added last so the final cuts feel cohesive and on-brand across formats.

Image retouching

Listing photos must show true product texture and color. Masks are used to adjust specific areas with precision — for example, correcting a reddish cast on skin, or adding subtle shadows around hair so individual strands read clearly.
