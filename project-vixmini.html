VIXMINI

VIXMINI is a hair care brand established in 2022 as a sub-brand of GL.iNet, specializing in high-performance straighteners and curlers designed to help users create unique, effortless styles. For this project, I created a series of visual assets for Amazon listings and social media platforms. To boost engagement and effectively highlight the products, we onboarded models to produce dynamic demonstration videos.

Blanca

Features digitally controlled precision heating (320–460°F) tailored for all hair types and textures. Equipped with 3D ceramic floating plates, it glides effortlessly through hair for a gentle, snag-free finish.

Ariella

Powered by advanced tourmaline ceramic floating plates and rapid PTC heating technology, Ariella delivers effortless, one-pass salon results. Its premium soft-touch exterior offers an elevated, comfortable styling experience.
