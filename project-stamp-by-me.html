Stamp By Me

Overview

Stamp By Me is an interactive museum experience designed for the Hong Kong Museum of Art. Using Bluetooth iBeacon technology, visitors move floor by floor, complete trivia challenges, and collect digital stamps and rewards.

Role & deliverables

  • UX/UI design for the interactive journey
  • Visual system for stamps, floors, and rewards
  • Content structure for trivia and guidance
  • Coordination with museum spatial flow

Approach

The interface stays clear and welcoming so visitors of all ages can follow the path, complete challenges, and feel a sense of progress as they explore the museum.
