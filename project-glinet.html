GL.iNet Promo Video

Overview

This promotional video introduces parental control features on GL.iNet routers. The aim was to explain the benefits clearly and make the product feel approachable for families and network administrators.

Role & deliverables

  • Storyboard and narrative structure
  • Motion graphics and visual treatment
  • On-screen typography and UI callouts
  • Final promotional cut for product marketing

Approach

I kept the pacing steady and the message simple: what the feature does, why it matters, and how it fits into everyday use. Motion and graphics support the story without overpowering the product.

Related work includes the OpenVPN setup tutorial and other GL.iNet product videos.
